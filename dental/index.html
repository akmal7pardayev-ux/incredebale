質問Questions people ask in pain
Will it hurt?

We numb the area with gel before any injection, then inject slowly. Most patients say they barely felt it. If anything feels wrong you raise your hand and we stop. Always.

Can you really see me today?

Yes. We keep six emergency times free every day for people in pain. If they're full, call us and we'll still find a way to see you, even if it's for a short pain-relief visit.

Will you pull my tooth out?

Only if you want us to, or if there's truly no other option. Saving the tooth is our first choice, and we'll explain both options and their prices.

I don't have insurance. What will it cost?

The emergency exam and X-ray start at $45. You'll see every price before treatment, and anything over $300 can be paid in three interest-free parts.

It's the middle of the night. What do I do?

Call our 24/7 line. A dentist will talk you through it and book you for the first time in the morning. If swelling spreads toward your eye or neck, or you find it hard to breathe or swallow, go to the emergency room straight away.
